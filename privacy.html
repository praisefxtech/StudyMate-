StudyMate Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Thank you for using StudyMate. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how StudyMate collects, uses and protects your information.

Information We Collect

StudyMate stores your study notes, goals, tasks and settings locally on your device. We do not collect or transmit your personal study data to our servers.

Permissions

StudyMate may request permission for notifications to remind you about study sessions and important tasks. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

Advertisements

Future versions of StudyMate may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect anonymous information to provide relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure. Password-protected notes remain stored only on your device unless future cloud backup features are introduced.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as new features are added. The latest version will always be available within the application.
